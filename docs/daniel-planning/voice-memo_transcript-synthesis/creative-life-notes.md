## Adjacent Project Ideas

### Childhood sketch to playable game engine
From the Jul 4 Bellingham drive. Concept: turn your childhood colored-pencil game and level sketches into playable, Super Nintendo style games, played with an actual gamepad through a web app. Proposed workflow: build the core engine first as a Claude/Fable code artifact to get powerful pieces working, then hand off to VS Code for production work. Needs research into existing open-source game engines well-regarded in the "creative code" community as a possible base. Open creative question you named directly: how literally to preserve the original colored-pencil aesthetic versus reimagining it the way the sketch's creator (young you) was actually imagining it at the time.

### Signal Bench / Tap, further ideation
Also from the Jul 4 drive, and consistent with what's already been built in this area. You wanted Fable to help explain the practical difference between raw FFT filtering and what Meyda does differently for audio feature extraction, survey existing tools for Syphon-input use cases, and learn ISF shaders before pushing further. Near-term deliverable you described: a browser tool that listens to a microphone across a few musical styles and outputs discrete, real-time variables you can map to visual parameters, with an open question about when you actually need isolated tracks (stems) or multiple physical mic inputs versus a single mixed signal.

### Artist CSA / "Curious Wizardry" (Jul 17, river session)
A shared storefront and community surface spanning everything under Curious Imagery, not just Fold: the game-engine idea, Fathom, Drift, a print subscription, custom visualizer commissions. Supporters would vote on what you build next, get alpha access, and get behind-the-scenes content. You framed this partly as a business idea and partly as a direct answer to something you named as a real cost: most of your breakthroughs happen alone, more than is healthy, and staying that isolated also forfeits what you called "currency," the goodwill of people who remember the earlier, harder version of a project and feel invested in where it went.

### Premium commissioned content business model
Covered in full in the Fold GTM doc, since it's the same underlying capability, but worth noting here too: this is really a creative-services business idea (get hired by a musician you're excited about for a multi-month embedded project), not a Fold feature or SKU.

### Open meta-request, not yet done
In the Jul 4 drive you asked, in passing, for a larger cross-project synthesis: a "soul searching distillation" comparing Drift, Fathom, Zoetrope, Fold, and something referred to as "Tend," cross-referenced against your "what else" notes and "sparks and seeds" notes, to surface what's common, what's diverging, and where the friction points are (creative collaboration, financial feedback loops, focus, publishing enough). That's a distinct and larger exercise from the four docs built today. Flagging it here so it doesn't get lost, not attempting it as part of this synthesis.

## Personal Notes and Reflections

### On mission and purpose (Jul 17, riverside)
This is the clearest and most repeated thread across sessions, worth preserving close to verbatim:

> "How do we put something in service to beauty? How do we put something in service to that twinkle in the eye, to restoring childlike wonder and connection?"

You framed it as building complex, difficult technical systems, but aiming them at restoring rather than extracting, using the same tools that have been optimized to ruthlessly extract attention and value elsewhere. You connected this explicitly across the Zoetrope, the game-creation idea, Fathom, Drift, and Fold. Tied to reading "This Here Flesh," and its idea that a childlike posture doesn't erase suffering but takes the edge off it.

### On isolation and sharing (Jul 17)
A direct, unprompted piece of self-reflection: most of the real breakthroughs and frustrating late nights have happened more alone than is good for you, both for your own wellbeing and because not sharing in real time gives up the goodwill of people who'd otherwise feel part of the process. This is part of what's behind the artist CSA idea above, not a separate thought.

### Process note
Stated preference, in passing: prototype heavily inside a Claude/Fable project as code artifacts to get strong pieces working before moving into VS Code for production.
